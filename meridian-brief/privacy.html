Privacy Policy for Meridian Brief

Last updated: November 2026

Meridian Brief ("we", "our", or the "App") respects your privacy. This Privacy Policy explains how information is handled when you use the Meridian Brief Android application (com.aistudio.meridianbrief.qxvwzt).

1. Information We Collect and Use

Google Sign-In & Account Information: When you sign in with Google via Firebase Authentication, we receive your basic profile identifiers (such as your name, email address, and unique user ID) solely to authenticate your account and keep your saved items private to you.

App Activity You Save: Bookmarked briefings, watched market tickers, tracked visa milestones, recent searches, and alert/theme preferences are stored locally on your device and synced to your private Google Cloud Firestore storage so your data persists across sessions.

Search & AI Verification Queries: Topics, tickers, and article URLs you submit to the Verify, Markets, or Article Brief features are sent securely through Firebase AI Logic to retrieve search-grounded summaries.

Optional Notifications: If you enable news alerts, the App requests notification permission (POST_NOTIFICATIONS) solely to deliver local alerts for new official briefings.

2. Third-Party Services

Meridian Brief uses Google Firebase (Authentication, Cloud Firestore, App Check, and Firebase AI Logic) to provide sign-in, cloud sync, security verification, and grounded summarization, and fetches public data from official government/regulatory feeds and financial chart endpoints. The App does not use third-party advertising SDKs and does not sell your personal data.

3. Data Retention & Deletion

You can remove bookmarks, tracked visas, watched tickers, and recent searches at any time directly inside the App. To request deletion of your account data stored in Cloud Firestore, contact us at sandeep.ethakotadeveloper@gmail.com.

4. Children's Privacy

Meridian Brief is not directed to children under 13 and does not knowingly collect personal information from children.

5. Contact Us

For privacy questions or data deletion requests, contact: sandeep.ethakotadeveloper@gmail.com.
