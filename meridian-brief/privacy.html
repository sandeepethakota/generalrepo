Privacy Policy

Effective Date: November 21, 2026 · App: Meridian Brief (com.aistudio.meridianbrief.qxvwzt)

1. Overview

Meridian Brief is published by sandeepe. We respect your privacy and collect only the minimum information needed to provide optional account sync and on-demand verification briefs.

You can use Meridian Brief in Guest Mode without signing in or providing any personal information.

2. Data We Collect & How It Is Used

  • Optional Google Sign-In (Firebase Authentication): If you choose to sign in with Google, we receive your basic Google account identifier (UID, display name, and email address) solely to authenticate your session and isolate your synced data.
  • User-Saved Preferences & Bookmarks (Cloud Firestore): When signed in, your saved briefings, tracked visa timelines, and watched market tickers are stored privately under your user ID in Firebase Cloud Firestore so they remain available across devices. In Guest Mode, saved items stay strictly on your device.
  • On-Demand AI Summaries & Verification (Firebase AI Logic): When you explicitly request an AI brief or source verification query, your query text is sent securely through Firebase AI Logic to generate a grounded summary. We do not sell your queries or use them for advertising.
  • App Integrity (Firebase App Check): Used to verify that requests originate from genuine installations of Meridian Brief.
  • Local Storage: Theme preference, notification settings, recent search history, and offline caches are stored locally on your device.

3. Data Sharing & Advertising

Meridian Brief does not sell personal data, does not include third-party ad networks, and does not share your saved items with other users. Data is processed only by Google Firebase infrastructure solely to operate authentication, database sync, App Check, and requested AI summaries.

4. Account & Data Deletion

You can remove saved bookmarks, visa trackers, and watched tickers directly inside the app, clear local data by uninstalling the app, or sign out at any time from Settings.

To request permanent deletion of your Firebase Authentication account and all associated Cloud Firestore data, email sandeep.ethakotadeveloper@gmail.com with the subject line "Meridian Brief Data Deletion Request" from the email address associated with your account. Requests are processed within 7 days.

5. Contact Us

If you have any questions about this Privacy Policy, contact us at sandeep.ethakotadeveloper@gmail.com.
