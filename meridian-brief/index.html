Editorial Standards & Official Sources

Meridian Brief operates as a transparent news and policy aggregator focused on verifiable primary records and established reporting:

  • Clear Attribution: Every briefing displays the originating publication or agency name, source domain, publication date, and direct link to the official source portal.
  • Official Government & Public Sources:
    • U.S. Federal Register: https://www.federalregister.gov/
    • U.S. Citizenship and Immigration Services (USCIS): https://www.uscis.gov/
    • U.S. Department of State (Visas & Visa Bulletin): https://travel.state.gov/
    • U.S. Department of Labor (PERM & Foreign Labor): https://www.dol.gov/
    • Board of Governors of the Federal Reserve System: https://www.federalreserve.gov/
    • U.S. Securities and Exchange Commission (SEC): https://www.sec.gov/
    • Federal Reserve Economic Data (FRED): https://fred.stlouisfed.org/
  • Corrections & Removal Requests: Publishers or readers who spot an attribution issue, broken source link, or factual concern can email sandeep.ethakotadeveloper@gmail.com for prompt review and correction.
